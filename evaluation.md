# Project Evaluation

## Overall Assessment

This AI-assisted portfolio review examines a 13-exchange business-conversation simulation. The user supplied the conversation; the stakeholder responses were generated by AI. The review documents observable evidence and limitations rather than claiming an independent skills assessment or employer score.

## Evidence Review

| Area | Observable evidence |
|---|---|
| Scenario coverage | Questions addressed products, cities, channels, customers, operations, and complaints. |
| Analytical questioning | Requested comparisons, averages, customer types, and additional data. |
| Business reasoning | Explored links between discounts, fulfillment problems, and repeat customers; causation remains unproven. |
| Data interpretation | Checked revenue and order trends while requesting profit-margin data. |
| Bilingual communication | Used Saudi Arabic for discovery and English for deeper analysis; this sample alone does not establish fluency or dialect proficiency. |
| Logical flow | Progressed from descriptive data to hypotheses, missing data, priorities, and recommendations. |
| Structure | Contains 13 analyst questions and 13 stakeholder responses, followed by a summary and recommendations. |

## Consistency Checks

- Product totals equal the monthly totals: 420,000 SAR in August and 505,000 SAR in September.
- City totals equal the monthly totals.
- Channel totals equal the monthly totals.
- Average order value is consistent with revenue divided by orders: approximately 339 SAR and 353 SAR.
- Complaint percentages are consistent with the total complaint count.
- The final recommendations are connected to the evidence instead of being generic.

## Corrections and Interpretation Limits

- The transcript is preserved as a sample for evaluation. Section 5 compares website revenue with **combined Instagram and WhatsApp** revenue; it cannot determine Instagram-only performance or overall channel effectiveness.
- The fall from 430 to 410 repeat customers is a change in counts, not a measured cohort retention rate.
- Complaint counts cover both months together. Statements about September or Abha complaint increases come from the simulated stakeholder narrative and cannot be verified from the aggregate table.
- The source narrative mentions higher discounts, delivery delays, and stock constraints. Their effect on revenue, repeat purchasing, or profit has not been established.
- The linked [SQL analysis](saudi-gift-store-sql/README.md) checks arithmetic, independent revenue breakdowns, and missing data while documenting these limitations.

## Strongest Evidence

The strongest analytical statement is the distinction between a leading hypothesis and a confirmed conclusion. The analysis also correctly identified the need for margin, cohort, stock-out, delivery, refund, marketing-cost, and complaint-rate data before making a final business decision.

## Improvement Opportunities

- Ask for conversion rate and website traffic before concluding that the website performs better.
- Compare complaint rates per order by city and channel, not only complaint counts.
- Separate the effects of discounts from the effects of free delivery.
- Include one or two additional Saudi Arabic questions to show more dialect writing evidence.
- When publishing, state clearly that the scenario and data are synthetic and self-directed.

## Resume-Ready Description

> AI-assisted self-directed portfolio sample: structured a 13-exchange Saudi Arabic and English business conversation using synthetic gift-store data, then reviewed consistency, missing data, and the limits of its recommendations.
